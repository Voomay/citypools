Recreation & Parks Department

Swimming Pools Schedule 2026/27

Community swimming pools reopen in phases during the summer season. This approach balances public recreation access with ongoing maintenance of critical municipal infrastructure.

Phase 1: September School Holidays (23 Sep - 5 Oct)
Open Monday to Sunday from 10:00 to 17:00. Note: Ticket sales close promptly at 16:30.
Phase 2: Off-Peak (6 Oct - 1 Dec 2026)
Open Wednesday to Friday 13:00 - 17:00, and Weekends 10:00 - 17:00. Closed Mondays & Tuesdays for filtration servicing.
Phase 3: Summer Peak Season (From 1 Dec 2026)
Open daily Monday to Sunday from 10:00 to 18:00.
⚠️ Please note: Re-opening dates may be adjusted due to operational requirements. Email enquiries: RP.enquiries@capetown.gov.za
